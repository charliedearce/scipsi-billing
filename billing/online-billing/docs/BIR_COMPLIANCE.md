### Source refresh and accountant handoff, 2026-09-20

A fresh review of the BIR-hosted RMC 77-2024 material, RR 26-2025 digest, EIS certification portal and RMC 77-2024 sample formats reconfirms the design boundary: a service sale needs the applicable invoice, while a payment receipt is supplementary; cash and credit do not remove the invoice requirement. The extended electronic-invoice date in S05 applies only after the taxpayer is classified within an applicable group. EIS portal availability does not establish this taxpayer's reporting obligation, usable schema, timing, production access or approval. [S02, S05, S07, S10]

This review supplied no company facts. P0-06 therefore remains open for the taxpayer's legal identity/branch scope, tax and activity classification, existing issuance evidence, and accountant-reviewed field/recognition/correction/retention matrix. The linked worksheet now contains the exact evidence-reference prompts. Do not enter raw TINs, credentials, customer data, BIR approval status or registration-history records into the repository.

RR 26-2025 names Small/Medium/Large e-commerce taxpayers, LTS taxpayers, EOPT Large taxpayers, and CAS/CBA with e-invoicing/other invoicing-software users in the extended group. Its e-commerce clause expressly excludes micro taxpayers; RR 11-2025 section 4 also contains micro exemptions for specified categories, including section 3(A)(4). Evaluate these together, not from one clause or a guessed turnover category. Exporter/RBE/POS status alone is not sufficient to assume the same activation date; overlapping software/LTS categories matter. [S04-S05]

**Working inference:** our planned billing software warrants early assessment under the invoicing-software category. Do not assume that being online automatically creates an EIS transmission obligation or that an exemption applies. Existing EIS participants may already have obligations: this plan does not suspend them.

The user's July 15, 2026 draft RMC and draft Annex A-1/A-2 remain reference material. Their final status, exact schemas and any proposed QR/Permit-to-Issue requirements were not verified in a final official publication during this research. Do not silently promote them into binding requirements or replace them with a similarly titled third-party article. Reserve versioned mapping support; obtain the final official issuance before implementation. RR 26-2025 itself was independently retrieved from BIR.

### Customer evidence, Document Studio, pricing, corrections, identity, communications and history (W20/W26/W28-W35)

Customer-uploaded withholding evidence is not a commercial discount. Approved W20 design separates eligibility from a particular certificate's accepted tax amount and allocates it by party, period and applicable tax type under a reviewed rule. Cash received and accepted withholding remain separate settlement components, with capacity/reversal controls preventing repeated certificate use. Form 2307 alone does not establish a universal percentage or calculation base. Late certificate review must preserve previously issued documents and actual funds. [S08]

Exemption review identifies the affected tax, legal basis, validity and covered transactions/services. VAT exemption, zero-rating and withholding exemption cannot share an undifferentiated boolean. Applicable treatment is captured in the fiscal invoice calculation and output; the collection receipt reflects that invoice. New approval/expiry cannot silently recalculate issued invoices or reprints. Taxpayer-specific evidence and rates remain P0-06 inputs. These W20 engineering controls were approved as design on 2026-09-19; S02/S09 and the applicable reviewed matrix remain their fiscal basis, not the user's approval alone.

The user approved W26: editable drafts with linked, authorized corrections/replacements for issued unpaid invoices, preserving the original number, PDF and history. Review the exact correction document and tax effects with accounting before implementing that path. Pending checkout, proof, check and credit/withholding allocations require reconciliation. Payment status does not by itself make an issued invoice editable. W24's approved instruction-start deadline is a collection control, not a fiscal issuance deadline; its check-clearance baseline does not settle provisional acknowledgment or tax-recognition treatment without accountant review.

W28 gives Administrators a controlled Studio, but no layout may hide mandatory fiscal content or turn a collection receipt into a sales invoice. W29 fuel surcharges are transparent snapshot components selected from tariff and scheduled fuel-policy versions; their tax/PPA interaction must come from the reviewed matrix, not a template or global checkbox. W30 late charges are post-due, separately linked VIP receivable events. The current design deliberately does not presume they are a tax penalty, interest, VATable/non-VAT, withholding-eligible or part of the original invoice; accountant review must select any fiscal document/series/template and GL/export treatment before P3-11 activation. W31 SMS carries only a generic, post-commit notification; neither queued/sent/provider-reported status nor a customer phone response proves a fiscal artifact was issued, delivered, accepted or paid. W32 company/full-name/email/mobile registration data and OTP are not a substitute for the buyer field/authority matrix: posting selects a reviewed buyer-profile version and preserves the issued snapshot. W33 serves only a safe app shell offline, while W34 notices are durable non-fiscal information. W35 requires append-only revisions/audit and linked corrections so issued invoice/receipt/artifact evidence remains reconstructable; history is evidence of what happened, not an automatic fiscal approval. The immutable portal/artifact and settlement records remain the evidence.

## 4. Architecture and Admin Settings

Add a **Fiscal Compliance** domain inside the Laravel application, not a separate financial database/service. It owns validated taxpayer/fiscal profile and rule versions and supplies posting validation. Billing remains owner of amounts and posting; Documents owns output; Integrations owns external submission; Accounting/Reporting owns reviewed exports. PostgreSQL remains authoritative, with real FKs to profile/rule versions and scoped constraints. Registration and approval workflows are not application modules.

Proposed records (not migrations): `fiscal_profile_versions`, `fiscal_rule_versions`, `fiscal_export_batches`, `submission_attempts`, `submission_acknowledgments`, and `retention_holds`. Reuse existing `document_series`, invoice snapshots, audit/outbox and artifacts rather than creating duplicate sales/balances. Scope every record by organization and relevant branch; never store signing secrets in ordinary settings. Do not add tables for registration applications, approval status or certificates merely to report whether an external action was completed.

Admin Settings > **Tax & BIR** should expose only system configuration and diagnostics:

- Taxpayer/branch profile values used on documents (legal name/address/TIN/branch, tax status and effective dates), separately from editable display branding. These are configuration inputs, not a registration-status record.
- Approved tax-code/rule versions, applicable invoice legends/identifiers and document series, using domain workflows rather than free-form key/value editing.
- Validation diagnostics showing missing or inconsistent values needed to issue a document. Do not show a “BIR registered/approved” flag.
- Separate electronic-issuance readiness and electronic-reporting activation, schema/environment, credential-reference status, failures and reconciliation queue. A gateway enable switch has no effect on BIR obligations.
- Records retention/holds and export access; prohibit reducing the applicable retention floor. Unknown filing anchors prevent automatic disposal. Five years is not a blanket permission to purge all records; other duties and unresolved cases require review.

Proposed permissions: `tax_profiles.view/manage`, `fiscal_rules.publish`, `fiscal_exports.generate`, `einvoice.configure/reconcile`, `retention_holds.manage`. Confirm exact names in P1. Use scoped authorization, optimistic versions, audit reasons and review for fiscal changes. Even Administrator cannot bypass mandatory fields, edit issued snapshots, reuse issued numbers, remove holds casually, or turn off a legal obligation. Disabling new transmissions for maintenance retains the durable backlog and obligation alerts.

For template fields such as ATP, acknowledgment, PTU or other identifiers, use the correct registered issuance mode and actual evidence. Do not print invented approval numbers, apply every registration field to every invoice type, or describe the app as BIR-certified merely because checks pass.
