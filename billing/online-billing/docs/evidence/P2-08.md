## Task Summary

Implemented and verified **Customer tax-evidence verification and treatment** ([Decision W20](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md), [`BIR_COMPLIANCE.md`](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md), [`PHASES.md`](../PHASES.md), and [`CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md`](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md)). Key deliverables:

- **Strict Separation of Withholding vs. Tax Exemption ([Decision W20](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - **Creditable Withholding Tax (BIR Form 2307)**:
    - Tracked in [`customer_withholding_certificates`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Models/CustomerWithholdingCertificate.php).
    - Captures payor/payee TIN and registered names, tax period (`period_from`, `period_to`), ATC code (e.g. `WC158`), income payment base, withholding rate, certified amount, and remaining unallocated balance (`remaining_amount`).
    - **Withholding Invariant**: BIR Form 2307 is treated strictly as a payment tender for settlement (Phase 3). It does **NOT** reduce invoice gross amounts or alter invoice VAT calculations.
  - **Tax-Specific Exemptions & Zero-Rating Rulings**:
    - Tracked in [`customer_tax_exemptions`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Models/CustomerTaxExemption.php).
    - Captures statutory legal basis (e.g. PEZA Law RA 7916, BOI, NIRC Section 109), ruling or certificate number (`ruling_or_cert_no`), covered services (`covered_services` array or `ALL`), validity date window (`valid_from`, `valid_to`), and exemption type (`ZERO_RATED` or `VAT_EXEMPT`).
- **Invoice Draft & Calculation Integration ([Decision W20, W29](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - In [`InvoiceDraftService::calculateDraft`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/InvoiceDraftService.php), line calculations evaluate [`TaxEvidenceService::findEffectiveExemption`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/TaxEvidenceService.php).
  - Matches the customer, business date, and service type or tariff code against active, approved exemptions.
  - When an approved exemption is found, line item tax treatment automatically resolves to `ZERO_RATED` or `EXEMPT` with `0.00` VAT, rather than the tariff's standard 12% VAT.
- **Fiscal Readiness Validation & Posting Gatekeeper ([Decision BIR-07, W20](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - In [`FiscalInvoiceService::validateFiscalReadiness`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/FiscalInvoiceService.php), any invoice draft with zero-rated or exempt lines is checked against customer exemptions.
  - If no approved, valid exemption covers the invoice business date, fiscal readiness validation fails with an error:
    `"Invoice contains non-VAT or zero-rated items, but customer has no approved tax exemption covering business date [{date}]."`
  - Posting via [`InvoicePostingService`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/InvoicePostingService.php) is blocked until a valid exemption is reviewed and approved.
- **Staff Review Workflows & Immutable Audit History**:
  - Review states: `PENDING_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, `NEEDS_CORRECTION`, `REJECTED`, `REVOKED`.
  - Approved withholding certificates initialize remaining balance (`remaining_amount = certified_amount`).
  - Immutable audit trail recorded in [`customer_tax_evidence_events`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Models/CustomerTaxEvidenceEvent.php) for every submission, decision, correction request, and revocation.
  - Real-time notification events broadcast to teller and customer channels via `DataRefreshEvent`.
- **Tenant & Customer Security**:
  - Cross-customer access attempts strictly blocked with HTTP 403 Forbidden.
  - Staff review and revocation endpoints protected with role permission checks (`billing:read`, `billing:draft`).
