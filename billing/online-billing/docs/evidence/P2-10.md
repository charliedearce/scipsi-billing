  - Tariff versions declare approved tax classification (`tax_treatment_key`: `VATABLE`, `EXEMPT`, `ZERO_RATED`, `NON_VAT`), PPA share applicability (`NOT_APPLICABLE` or `APPLICABLE` with `ppa_share_rate`), and fuel surcharge applicability (`NOT_APPLICABLE` or `APPLICABLE`).
  - Strict effective-date window validation prevents overlapping published/effective versions for the same tariff.
  - Future-dated scheduled activation supports Asia/Manila effectivity dates; server resolution activates versions based on invoice business date/time without manual intervention.

  - Implemented in [`FuelSurchargeController`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Http/Controllers/Api/V1/FuelSurchargeController.php).
  - Captures audited fuel price observations (`product_grade`, `price`, `currency`, `unit_of_measure`, `observed_at`, `effective_at`, `entered_by_user_id`, `notes`, `status`).
  - Admin endpoints to record and retire observations (`POST /admin/fuel/observations`, `POST /admin/fuel/observations/{id}/retire`).
  - Protected with `fuel_surcharges:view` and `fuel_surcharges:manage` permissions.
- **Fuel Surcharge Policies & Non-Overlapping Price Bands ([Decision W29](../discovery/PRICING_RULES.md))**:
  - Implemented policy versioning with price bands (`FuelSurchargePolicyVersion`, `FuelSurchargeBand`).
  - Strict mathematical validation ([`PricingResolutionService::validateBands`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/PricingResolutionService.php)):
    - Enforces non-overlapping price ranges: band `[min_price, max_price)` and adjacent band must not overlap.
    - Validates bounds: `min_price < max_price` when `max_price` is specified.
    - Supports at most one band with an open upper bound (`max_price === null`).
    - Supports explicit 0% surcharge band (`surcharge_percent = '0.0000'`) for subsidized/low-price tiers (not a missing configuration).
    - Enforces non-overlapping effective date windows for published policies.
- **Server-Side Pricing Resolution & Calculation Protocols ([Decision W29](../discovery/PRICING_RULES.md))**:
  - Resolves active tariff version, fuel observation, and policy version for the locked invoice business date/time.
  - Strict band boundary resolution: lower bound is inclusive (`min_price <= price`), upper bound is exclusive (`price < max_price`).
  - Rejects ambiguous or missing configuration with clear, structured `ValidationException` (never silently charges zero or reuses stale unapproved prices):
    - Missing fuel observation for fuel-applicable tariff halts calculation.
    - Missing fuel policy for fuel-applicable tariff halts calculation.
    - Observed fuel price falling outside configured bands halts calculation.
- **Per-Line Item Independence (No Global Checkbox Contamination)**:
  - Fuel surcharge and PPA share apply strictly per eligible line item.
  - Verified mixed-line invoices containing:
    - Line 1: Fuel APPLICABLE (5%) + PPA APPLICABLE (10%) + VATABLE (12%).
    - Line 2: Fuel NOT_APPLICABLE + PPA NOT_APPLICABLE + VATABLE (12%).
    - Fuel surcharge and PPA amount on Line 2 are strictly `0.00`, proving zero contamination across invoice items.
- **Immutable Issued Invoice Invariant**:
  - Invoices posted with pricing snapshots (rate, fuel surcharge amount/percent, PPA amount/rate) are **never** repriced when tariff versions, fuel observations, or surcharge policy versions are subsequently modified or retired.
