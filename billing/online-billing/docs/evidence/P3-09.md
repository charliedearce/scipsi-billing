## Implemented contract

- `invoice_credit_charges` now keeps both `charged_business_date` and `charged_at`. Future charges always set both; old/imported rows without a business date retain an explicit exception path.
- Aging is a read model, not a balance ledger: charge amount minus **POSTED** receipt allocations where `receipts.business_date <= as_of_date`. `receipts.posted_at` remains the recorded timestamp. The response labels the Asia/Manila cutoff semantics.
- Buckets are Current, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 91+, and **Unclassified - needs terms review**. A null imported due date is counted in reconciliation and never silently made Current or omitted. A blocking credit policy blocks a new charge while such debt remains unclassified.
- PPA receives current settlement and credit facts separately. `PAID`, `PARTIALLY_PAID` and `UNPAID` are never relabelled by VIP credit; unpaid charged debt is `ON_CREDIT`.
- The Admin PPA clearance policy is versioned, draft/publish controlled and safe by default: no active policy means full payment required. A published policy can return `ON_CREDIT_ACCEPTED` for an existing qualified credit assignment, but cannot post a receipt, change balance, issue a release, gate pass or formal clearance.
- Every PPA lookup writes a scoped `ppa_verification_events` audit record. PPA location scope is enforced from the invoice location.
