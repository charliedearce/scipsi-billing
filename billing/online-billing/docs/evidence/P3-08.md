## Implementation and evidence

- Added restrictive-FK PostgreSQL records for versioned organization credit policies, customer credit-account profile versions, one immutable credit assignment per invoice, explicit VIP repayment allocations/proof versions, credit-account events and reviewer-confirmed bank-transfer identities. The original invoice remains the only receivable; an `invoice_credit_charge` is term/exposure metadata, not a second invoice, payment or receipt.
- Administrators can draft/publish non-overlapping organization policies and publish effective customer profile versions. A capped limit needs a numerical amount; unlimited is an explicit mode. Profile overrides are rejected unless the active organization policy permits them. Account configuration, policy publication and events are audit logged with real foreign keys and expected-version controls.
- Only a linked, active customer explicitly classified `vip` with an active effective profile can charge fully unpaid posted bills. The database transaction locks account/charge/invoice state, rejects another credit assignment or pending payment proof, rechecks currency/current invoice version/overdue rule/exposure, captures policy/profile/terms/due date and records an account event. A held/disabled profile prevents new borrowing but not repayment of debt already assigned.
- VIP repayment keeps a separate proof queue because P3-05 regular customer instructions intentionally require full-balance payment and frozen deadline routing. A VIP submits explicit positive per-invoice amounts and clean proof; pending/rejected evidence changes neither invoice balance nor exposure. Authorized staff claim oldest work, make an all-or-nothing decision, and use `ReceiptPostingService` for the one immutable collection receipt/allocation transaction.
- A reviewer-confirmed bank reference is unique by organization/currency across manual and VIP bank-transfer channels. A proof file version is also not reusable. The duplicate guard is a reference identity, not a misleading image-hash identity. The regular P3-05 approval path now shares the same confirmed-reference guard when a reference is supplied.
- Customer **My VIP Credit**, Teller **VIP Repayment Review** and Administrator **VIP Credit & Collections** workspaces expose credit terms/exposure, charge selection, explicit partial repayment, reviewer receipt posting and versioned default policy publication. Reverb refreshes are best-effort wakeups only; server-side balances, credit eligibility and receipt posting remain authoritative.
- The feature deliberately does not claim a sale is paid when charged to credit, generate a receipt before verified bank funds, enable a gateway, define PPA clearance, calculate late charges, choose tax/GL treatment for late charges, send VIP SMS or introduce a receipt-reversal rule.
