## Implementation and evidence

- Legacy source paths/symbols and confidence: The legacy VB6 billing system had no customer self-service, no portal registration, and no OTP verification; customers were created manually by staff and printed bills were physically handed over. Implemented a decoupled identity, customer business account, explicit link, and buyer-profile version architecture fulfilling all Decision W32 requirements.
- Changed paths and resulting behavior:
  - Added migration `2026_09_19_100010_create_customers_and_buyer_profiles_tables.php` creating 5 FK-backed tables on PostgreSQL:
    - `customers`: Organization-scoped business accounts (`CUST-YYYY-XXXX`).
    - `customer_user_links`: Explicit authorization link between portal user and business account with roles (`owner`, `admin`, `billing_contact`, `member`) and active flag.
    - `customer_buyer_profiles`: Root aggregate for buyer billing identity with `current_version` pointer.
    - `buyer_profile_versions`: Versioned immutable snapshot container for registered name, trade name, TIN, branch code, tax classification, and billing address.
    - `contact_verification_challenges`: Purpose-bound OTP challenges (`REGISTRATION_MOBILE`, `MOBILE_CHANGE`, `WALK_IN_CLAIM`), salted SHA-256 code hash, 5-minute expiry, single-use atomic consumption, and 3-attempt lockout tracking.
    - Added `customer_id` FK to `customer_contact_points`.
  - Implemented Eloquent Models:
    - `Customer`, `CustomerUserLink`, `CustomerBuyerProfile`, `BuyerProfileVersion`, and `ContactVerificationChallenge`.
    - Updated `CustomerContactPoint` and `User` with cross-domain relationships.
  - Implemented Purpose-Bound OTP Service Layer:
    - `OtpGatewayInterface`, `OtpDispatchResult`, `FakeOtpGateway`, and `SkySmsOtpGateway`.
    - `OtpService`: Generates 6-digit random codes, stores salted SHA-256 hash (`hash('sha256', $code . $salt)`), enforces atomic single-use consumption (`consumed_at`), 5-minute expiry, and lockout after 3 incorrect attempts. Never stores or logs plaintext OTP codes.
  - Implemented Registration Service Layer:
    - `CustomerRegistrationService`: Validates the 4 user-mandated registration fields (`full_name`, `company_name`, `email`, `mobile`), normalizes Philippine mobile numbers to E.164 (`+639XXXXXXXXX`), creates the 4 distinct records in one database transaction, returns non-enumerating responses when duplicate active email is submitted, and verifies registration OTP to activate the user, link, customer account, and issue a Sanctum token.
  - Controllers & API Routes:
    - `CustomerRegistrationController`: `POST /api/v1/auth/register` (public, throttled, non-enumerating), `POST /api/v1/auth/register/resend`.
    - `ContactVerificationController`: `POST /api/v1/auth/contact-verifications/mobile/send` and `POST /api/v1/auth/contact-verifications/mobile/verify`.
    - `CustomerProfileController`: `GET /api/v1/portal/profile` and `PUT /api/v1/portal/profile` (audited, creates versioned drafts, never alters issued invoices/ORs).
    - `CustomerAccountAdminController`: `GET /api/v1/admin/customers`, `GET /api/v1/admin/customers/{id}`, `PUT /api/v1/admin/customers/{id}/status`, `GET /api/v1/admin/customers/{id}/buyer-profiles`, `POST /api/v1/admin/customers/{id}/buyer-profiles/{versionId}/review`.
  - Seeders & RBAC:
    - Updated `DatabaseSeeder` with W32 permissions (`customer_accounts:view/manage`, `buyer_profiles:view/edit/review`, `customer_contacts:view/manage`, `customer_registration:review`) and synchronized to `Administrator`, `Teller`, and `Customer` roles.
  - Frontend Workspaces (Vue 3 + TypeScript):
    - `apps/web/src/api/registration.ts`: Typed API helper functions.
    - `apps/web/src/views/auth/register/index.vue`: Complete rewrite supporting the 4 mandatory fields with input validation, followed by an interactive mobile OTP verification step featuring masked phone display (`+6391****567`), a 60-second resend countdown timer, and automatic portal authentication.
