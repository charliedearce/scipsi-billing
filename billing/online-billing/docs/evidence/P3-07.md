## Implemented read-only verification boundary

- `GET /api/v1/ppa/bills/{invoiceNumber}/settlement` remains guarded by `ppa:verify`, organization scope, and the PPA user's assigned location. Every successful check creates an append-only `ppa_verification_events` audit record with the current settlement, credit, policy, outstanding amount, actor, and server check time.
- Confirmed applied amounts and settlement status continue to count only allocations from `POSTED` receipts.
- The response includes up to the ten most recent confirmed (`POSTED`) collection-receipt allocations and, separately, up to the ten most recent `REVERSED` allocations for the requested invoice. Each summary contains only the receipt number, receipt business date, amount applied to that invoice, and `receipt_status`. It does not expose proof files, tender references, payer snapshots, receipt PDFs, or a receipt download link.
- Reversed receipts are history only: they never contribute to `applied_amount`, `outstanding_amount`, `settlement_status`, or `clearance_eligibility`.
- The PPA workspace shows invoice total, confirmed amount, outstanding amount, checked-at time, the confirmed-receipt table, and a reversed-receipt history section when any reverse exists. It continues to distinguish `PAID`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, and `UNPAID` from the separately labeled VIP on-credit policy result.
- The PPA role has no receipt-posting or teller-proof-review permission. The verification route creates neither a payment, receipt, clearance, release, gate pass, nor a realtime financial command.
