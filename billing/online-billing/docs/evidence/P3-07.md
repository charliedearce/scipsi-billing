## Implemented read-only verification boundary

- `GET /api/v1/ppa/bills/{invoiceNumber}/settlement` remains guarded by `ppa:verify`, organization scope, and the PPA user's assigned location. Every successful check creates an append-only `ppa_verification_events` audit record with the current settlement, credit, policy, outstanding amount, actor, and server check time.
- The response now includes the count and up to the ten most recent **posted** collection-receipt allocations for the requested invoice. Each summary contains only the receipt number, receipt business date, and amount applied to that invoice. It does not expose proof files, tender references, payer snapshots, receipt PDFs, or a receipt download link.
- The PPA workspace now shows invoice total, confirmed amount, outstanding amount, checked-at time, and the minimal confirmed-receipt table. It continues to distinguish `PAID`, `PARTIALLY_PAID`, and `UNPAID` from the separately labeled VIP on-credit policy result.
- The PPA role has no receipt-posting or teller-proof-review permission. The verification route creates neither a payment, receipt, clearance, release, gate pass, nor a realtime financial command.
