# P3-01 Evidence — Settlement equations

Date: 2026-09-20. Status: `AUTOMATED_VERIFIED` for the pure calculation contract; accounting/provider acceptance remains pending.

## Delivered

- Added `SettlementCalculatorService` using BCMath decimal strings and the existing T2 monetary convention.
- Separates confirmed cash, approved withholding, pending proof/clearance and rejected amounts.
- Applies cash before withholding, preserves certificate capacity when cash already covers the balance, and reports unapplied surplus/overpayment explicitly.
- Treats cleared checks as eligible and pending checks as non-settling until clearance.
- Rejects duplicate withholding certificate applications and applications above remaining certificate capacity.
- Preserves actual partial verified collections while rejecting regular-customer partial checkout selections under W23.
- Added the reusable equation and P3-02 integration contract in [SETTLEMENT_EQUATIONS.md](../discovery/SETTLEMENT_EQUATIONS.md).
