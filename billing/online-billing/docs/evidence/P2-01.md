# P2-01 Evidence — Build Draft and Calculation Flow

Date: 2026-09-19. Status: AUTOMATED_VERIFIED.

## Task Summary

Implemented the core invoice drafting and server-authoritative decimal calculation engine per Phase 2 specifications (Decisions W28–W30, W35, [`PRICING_RULES.md`](../discovery/PRICING_RULES.md), [`CALCULATION_FIXTURES.md`](../discovery/CALCULATION_FIXTURES.md), and [`BIR_COMPLIANCE.md`](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md)). Key deliverables:

- **Relational Persistence & Versioned Pricing Snapshots**:
  - `tariffs`: Organization-scoped tariff master specifying service types (`ARRASTRE`, `STEVEDORING`, `OTHER`), route types (`DOMESTIC`, `FOREIGN`), and units of measure.
  - `tariff_versions`: Versioned, effective-dated tariffs declaring tax treatment (`VATABLE`, `EXEMPT`, `ZERO_RATED`), PPA share applicability and rates, and fuel surcharge applicability.
  - `fuel_price_observations`: Dated fuel price entries with organization scope and currency/unit specifications.
  - `fuel_surcharge_policy_versions` & `fuel_surcharge_bands`: Non-overlapping price bands with explicit lower inclusive and upper exclusive boundaries (including open upper bounds) determining surcharge percentages.
  - `invoices`: Draft and issued invoice headers recording organization tenancy, customer link, buyer profile version, currency, line totals, and BIR fiscal readiness indicators.
  - `invoice_items`: Normalized line items with description, quantity, unit rate, and calculated amounts.
  - `invoice_item_pricing_snapshots`: Immutable snapshot frozen per line item capturing the exact tariff version, fuel observation, matching band, and calculation payload used at calculation time.
- **Exact Decimal Calculation Engine (`DecimalCalculatorService`)**:
  - Pure `bcmath` arithmetic avoiding native floating-point representations.
  - Implements `T2` truncation toward zero to 2 decimal places for monetary amounts and 4 decimal places for rates/quantities.
  - Matches reference arithmetic from P0-03 synthetic fixtures (`fixtures/legacy-calculations.json`).
  - Supports mixed invoice lines (VATable, exempt, zero-rated, PPA-applicable, and fuel-surcharge-applicable) without bill-wide checkbox side effects.
- **Customer & Buyer Profile Validation (`BuyerProfileValidationService`)**:
  - Enforces active `Customer` and active `BuyerProfileVersion` requirements.
  - Implements P0-06 boundary: Missing BIR fiscal fields (`tin`, `registered_name`, `branch_code`, `billing_address`) mark `is_fiscal_ready = false` and list missing fields, but do **not** prevent creating or updating preliminary drafts.
- **Concurrency & Document History Integration (Decision W35 / P1-13)**:
  - Draft updates require `expected_version`. Stale edits receive HTTP 409 Conflict.
  - Integrates `DocumentRevisionService`: Every draft creation and update records an append-only revision in `document_revisions` with SHA-256 snapshot hashes and reason codes.
- **REST Endpoints & Controllers**:
  - `POST /api/v1/invoices/calculate`: Pre-flight calculation preview without persisting.
  - `POST /api/v1/invoices/drafts`: Create draft invoice with initial document revision.
  - `GET /api/v1/invoices/drafts`: Paginated draft list.
  - `GET /api/v1/invoices/drafts/{id}`: Draft detail with frozen line pricing snapshots and buyer profile validation.
  - `PUT /api/v1/invoices/drafts/{id}`: Concurrency-checked draft update with audit revision.
  - `GET /api/v1/tariffs`: Active tariffs with effective pricing versions.

## Boundaries & Non-Functional Guarantees Preserved

1. **No Silent Floating-Point Math**: Native float arithmetic is strictly forbidden. All monetary math uses `bcmath`.
2. **Draft vs. Fiscal Posting**: Drafts have null `invoice_number` and status `DRAFT`. They do not consume registered fiscal numbers or represent statutory invoice issuance until P2-02 posting.
3. **No Automatic Repricing**: Saved draft lines and snapshots remain immutable unless an explicit update/recalculation request is submitted.
