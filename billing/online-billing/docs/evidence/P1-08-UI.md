## Implementation and evidence

- Legacy: none (new digital chat). Replaced Art Design Pro demo “Art Bot” / mock notice panel with durable Conversation + InAppNotification APIs over Laravel Reverb.
- Behavior:
  - `InAppNotificationPublisher` persists + after-commit `InAppNotificationEvent` wake-up; billing/tax/claim/payment/VIP creators routed through it.
  - One open conversation per billing request; find-or-create on teller claim and `POST /conversations/for-billing-request/{id}`.
  - Customer-visible messages notify other participants; staff notes do not notify customers.
  - Vue: global Echo bootstrap after `/auth/me`, header unread badges, real notification panel, chat drawer + `/chat` page, entry points on teller queue and customer request detail.
- Financial effects: none. Chat cannot post invoices, allocate payments, or change balances.
