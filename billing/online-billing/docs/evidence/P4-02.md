# P4-02 — Statements and transmittals

## Implemented statement slice

- `account_statements` and `account_statement_items` hold immutable as-of receivables snapshots with restrictive foreign keys to customer and invoice facts.
- A snapshot includes only the customer's `POSTED` invoices dated on/before the as-of date and effective `POSTED` receipt allocations dated on/before that date. Fully settled invoices are excluded; invoice, applied-payment, and outstanding figures are captured per included invoice using BCMath.
- Snapshot generation locks the customer and relevant invoices, records a non-fiscal `SOA-<UUID>` reference and a structured customer snapshot, and emits an audit event. It cannot post payment, affect a receipt, settle an invoice, create late charges, or alter a fiscal document.
- Teller/Admin **Account Statements** supports selecting an active customer, choosing an as-of date, generating a snapshot, viewing its frozen lines, and authenticated download of the canonical PDF.

## Implemented transmittal slice

- `transmittals`, `yellow_transmittal_items`, and `white_transmittal_items` preserve real restrictive foreign keys to their header and invoice/receipt source facts. Separate item tables intentionally avoid an unsafe polymorphic source reference.
- A yellow transmittal captures an operator-selected, distinct set of `POSTED` invoices that are in the caller organization/location and dated on or before the chosen as-of date. It freezes invoice number/date/currency/total and the issued buyer snapshot.
- A white transmittal uses the same explicit-membership/as-of rule for `POSTED` receipts and freezes receipt number/date/currency, payer snapshot, cash/withholding/applied/unapplied figures and source status/version facts. Where an existing receipt header has no location, it is eligible only when all allocated invoices are in the selected location; a mixed-location receipt is excluded.
- Generation locks selected source rows in a database transaction, rejects duplicate, unavailable, post-as-of, cross-location, cross-organization, and mixed-currency selections, produces a non-fiscal `YTR-` or `WTR-` ULID reference, and writes a `TRANSMITTAL_GENERATED` audit event. It cannot post/settle/reverse a source document or consume a fiscal number.
- Teller/Admin **Yellow & White Transmittals** loads bounded eligible source summaries, explicitly selects source documents, generates a snapshot, views frozen membership, and downloads the canonical PDF. The route contracts are recorded in `contracts/openapi.yaml`.
- The legacy white-transmittal BIR 2307/income/withholding fields are deliberately not computed or labelled in this implementation. The current receipt model does not provide an accountant-approved equivalent; no tax conclusion is inferred from receipt figures.

## Canonical non-fiscal output slice

- Statement and transmittal generation writes a `DocumentSnapshot` whose payload contains only the already-frozen statement/transmittal facts, then renders a private `DocumentArtifact` PDF from that payload. Rendering does not query live invoices, receipts, allocations, tariffs, customer data, or policy values.
- The seeded, globally activated system templates are `SOA-DEFAULT` (`ACCOUNT_STATEMENT`), `YTR-DEFAULT` (`YELLOW_INVOICE`), and `WTR-DEFAULT` (`WHITE_RECEIPT`). Admin Document Studio can create, preview, validate structurally, publish, and activate those non-fiscal layout kinds; current protected-field validation remains limited to the three fiscal sales-invoice kinds.
- Artifact bytes have SHA-256 integrity data and a pinned renderer identifier; files are private and accessible only through organization/location-scoped authenticated download endpoints. A failed artifact may be retried only from the stored template version and stored payload. It never recalculates a balance or changes a financial/source record.
- These PDFs are expressly non-fiscal operational communications. They do not issue, amend, settle, or replace an invoice, collection receipt, OR, BIR form, or payment proof. Physical printer output remains deferred.
