## Implementation and evidence

- Added restrictive-FK-backed `payment_policy_versions`, `payment_groups`, `payment_group_items` and append-only `payment_group_events`. A group captures its customer, selected full balances/expected invoice versions, policy version, currency, frozen instructions, instruction deadline and operational-target snapshots. A nullable link from the P3-05 proof ledger preserves migration compatibility; current proof submission/review requires a payment-group snapshot.
- Administrators can draft and publish non-overlapping policy versions with an optimistic lock. The Admin **Payment Routes & Deadlines** workspace manages the policy. No sample threshold, deadline, bank account or gateway is activated by code; an administrator must publish an operational policy.
- A linked customer can create one account/currency manual instruction group only from posted invoices at their full current remaining balances. The threshold is the aggregate selected gross remaining balance before this group's withholding and excluding fees. Equality is manual; only a strictly lower selection is gateway-eligible. Because P3-06 has no verified provider route, gateway-eligible selections are blocked rather than silently changing channel.
- Payment deadlines freeze at instruction issuance and do not reset on proof resubmission. Review targets begin on each submitted proof; deposited-check clearance targets begin when the teller claims the proof; correction windows begin on rejection. A correction-window expiry changes only the group to `EXPIRED`, leaves the invoice debt/aging untouched and permits a new instruction. Late proof remains submitted for reconciliation rather than being deleted.
- Customers choose bank transfer or check deposit. A claimed check proof requires the separate `checks:confirm_clearance` permission and an assigned-teller/version check to record an auditable `CLEARED` or `DISHONORED` decision. Collection receipt posting is blocked unless the check is `CLEARED`; when posted, the immutable receipt tender is `CHECK`/`CLEARED`, never mislabeled as a bank transfer.
- Customer and Teller Vue workspaces show the frozen method/deadline/targets and expose check-clearance actions only during assigned review. Reverb refresh events remain best-effort and do not decide balances, receipt posting or clearance.
