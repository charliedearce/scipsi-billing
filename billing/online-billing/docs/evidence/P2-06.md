## Task Summary

Implemented and verified the **Fiscal Invoice and Structured-Data Contract** ([Decisions W19, W28, W32, W35](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md), [`BIR_COMPLIANCE.md`](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md), and [`PHASES.md`](../PHASES.md)). Key deliverables:

- **Versioned Taxpayer Issuer Profile (`TaxpayerProfileVersion`)**:
  - Organization-scoped versioned legal identity storing legal corporate name, trade name, TIN, branch code (`00000`), tax classification (`VAT_REGISTERED`), RDO code (`111`), registered physical address, line of business, BIR CAS Permit Number (`BIR-CAS-2026-00129-GENSAN`), permit issuance date, statutory legend (`VAT SALES INVOICE`), and effective timestamp ranges.
  - Active profile scope with automatic resolution for billing and reporting.
- **Fiscal Tax Classification Rules (`FiscalTaxRuleVersion`)**:
  - Versioned statutory tax rules across all four standard classifications:
    - `VATABLE`: 12.00% VAT rate, buyer TIN required per RA 11976 / RMC 77-2024, legend "VATable Sales", legal basis "NIRC Sec. 108".
    - `ZERO_RATED`: 0.00% rate, buyer TIN required, legend "Zero-Rated Sales pursuant to NIRC Sec. 108(B)", legal basis "NIRC Sec. 108(B)".
    - `EXEMPT`: 0.00% rate, legend "VAT-Exempt Sales under NIRC Sec. 109", legal basis "NIRC Sec. 109".
    - `NON_VAT`: 0.00% rate, legend "Non-VAT Sales subject to Percentage Tax under NIRC Sec. 116", legal basis "NIRC Sec. 116".
- **Immutable Issuer Snapshot Capture on Posting**:
  - `InvoicePostingService` requires an active `TaxpayerProfileVersion` to post. If missing, posting is rejected with HTTP 422.
  - On commit, permanently snapshots the issuer's registered name, trade name, TIN, branch code, tax classification, address, and permit number onto the `invoices` table (`issuer_snapshot_*`) alongside the immutable buyer snapshot (`buyer_snapshot_*`).
- **Standard Machine-Readable Structured Fiscal Data Contract (`FiscalInvoiceService`)**:
  - Generates canonical JSON conforming to the Philippine BIR EOPT / RR 11-2025 / RR 26-2025 structured extract readiness:
    - `schema_version`: `"1.0.0"`
    - `standard`: `"BIR-EOPT-CAS-INVOICE"`
    - `document_kind`: `"SALES_INVOICE"`
    - Complete issuer and buyer immutable identity profiles.
    - Explicit tax breakdown: `vatable_sales`, `zero_rated_sales`, `vat_exempt_sales`, `vat_amount`, `ppa_share_amount`, `fuel_surcharge_amount`, and `total_amount_due`.
    - Detailed line items with unit rates, gross amounts, fuel surcharges, PPA shares, and line-level tax classification.
    - Linked canonical artifact verification (file existence, byte size, and SHA-256 integrity hash).
- **Exact Mathematical Totals Reconciliation**:
  - Validates that `total_amount_due` exactly matches the sum of line items.
  - Verifies zero variance across total charges, VAT amount, fuel surcharge, PPA share, and net taxable amounts.
  - Flags reconciliation status as `RECONCILED` (`is_balanced = true`) or `DISCREPANCY`.
- **REST Endpoints & Security**:
  - `GET /api/v1/invoices/{id}/fiscal-data`: Machine-readable structured fiscal data with tenant isolation (foreign customer access denied with 403 Forbidden).
  - `GET /api/v1/fiscal/taxpayer-profile`: Returns active issuer profile.
  - `GET /api/v1/fiscal/tax-rules`: Returns active fiscal tax classification rules.
