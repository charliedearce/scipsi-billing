## Task Summary

Implemented and verified **Selected-bill walk-in and verified counter claims workflow** ([Decision W25](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md), [`BIR_COMPLIANCE.md`](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md), [`PHASES.md`](../PHASES.md), and [`CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS.md`](../discovery/CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS.md)). Key deliverables:

- **Walk-in Customer & Invoice Isolation ([Decision W25](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - Implemented [`WalkInCustomer`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Models/WalkInCustomer.php) and [`WalkInBillingService`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/WalkInBillingService.php).
  - Walk-in counter transactions create an audited walk-in record with captured buyer details (name, TIN, branch code, registered address, mobile, email) and a dedicated internal shell `Customer` record (`customer_type = WALK_IN`) for invoice foreign-key integrity.
  - The `customer_id` on the walk-in record remains `null` until linked via an approved claim or explicit staff link.
  - **Immutable Buyer Snapshot Invariant**: When a walk-in invoice is posted and later claimed, the issued buyer snapshot on the posted invoice is **NEVER** modified.
- **Invoice Number Claim Initiation & Route Determination ([Decision W25](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - Implemented [`BillClaimService::initiateClaim`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/BillClaimService.php).
  - **Blind Number Lookup**: Non-existent or unmatched invoice numbers return generic responses and route to `TELLER_REVIEW` without disclosing whether the invoice number exists.
  - **Rate Limiting**: Per-user rate-limiting window (10 attempts per 60 minutes) protects against brute-force enumeration of invoice numbers.
  - **Automatic Route Determination**:
    - **`CLAIM_CODE` route**: Selected when the requesting portal customer has an approved, verified contact point (`mobile` or `email`) matching the contact point captured on the walk-in billing record.
    - **`TELLER_REVIEW` route**: Selected when no matching verified contact point exists, routing to staff review.
- **Cryptographic Claim Code Security ([Decision W25, W31](../discovery/CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md))**:
  - 6-digit cryptographically secure random codes (`random_int`), single-use, 15-minute TTL.
  - **Purpose Prefix Invariant**: Hash calculation uses purpose prefix `BILL_CLAIM_` with a 32-character random salt (`hash('sha256', 'BILL_CLAIM_' . $rawCode . $salt)`). Registration or mobile-change OTPs cannot be recycled as claim codes.
  - **Information Withholding**: `code_hash` and `code_salt` are hidden from serialization (`makeHidden`) and never exposed to the client.
  - **Transactional SMS Dispatch**: Code is dispatched via after-commit SMS notification intent without persisting or logging the raw code in cleartext.
- **Verification, Lockout & Constant-Time Comparison**:
  - Verification in [`BillClaimService::verifyClaimCode`](file:///h:/SCIPSI-BILLING/billing/online-billing/apps/api/app/Services/Billing/BillClaimService.php) uses `hash_equals` for constant-time hash comparison to prevent timing attacks.
  - Atomic attempt counter tracks attempts up to `max_attempts` (5). If exceeded, the claim transitions to `REJECTED` status.
  - Attempt increments and failure audit events persist across invalid verification attempts.
- **Staff Decision & Non-Destructive Linking**:
  - Staff (Teller or Admin with `bill_claims:review` permission) can review and decide `PENDING_TELLER_REVIEW` claims (`APPROVE` or `REJECT` with mandatory reason).
  - On approval (via verified code or staff decision), the claim links the walk-in record to the portal customer account (`WalkInBillingService::linkWalkInToPortalCustomer`), dispatches in-app notifications, and broadcasts `DataRefreshEvent`.
  - Re-linking guard ensures a walk-in record cannot be claimed by a different portal customer once linked.
