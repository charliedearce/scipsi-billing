## 2026-10-05 follow-up: receipt reversal after a voided charge

- Reproduced the gap with the real proof approval, receipt reversal, and aging API path: after the approved reversal, the assessment stayed `VOIDED` even though principal was outstanding again. The new test failed on that status before the fix.
- `ReceiptReversalService` now calls `LateChargeAssessmentService` after changing the receipt to `REVERSED`, within the reversal transaction. Only assessments voided because a payment was effective by the assessment date are checked. If posted funds no longer cover that date's principal, the assessment becomes `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` with a receipt-linked late-charge event and audit event. A retry produces no second event or assessment. No charge is automatically posted and the original invoice terms remain intact.
- Admin VIP Credit & Collections can filter `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` assessments for review. The Teller VIP proof queue displays the first-submitted timestamp and states that pending proof does not change due dates or principal aging. This adds visibility without inventing an automatic proof expiry or bank decision.
