## Implementation and evidence

- Added organization-scoped manual-payment submissions, explicit selected invoice rows, immutable proof-attempt/version rows and append-only proof events. All financial/customer/file/receipt links use restrictive foreign keys; an exact private-file version can be attached to only one submission.
- Customer endpoints expose posted bills with current settled/outstanding amounts and receipt history, permit an active linked user to submit a clean `PAYMENT_PROOF` against one or more full current balances, show only that user's submissions, and permit a rejected proof to be resubmitted with a newer clean version while retaining the original queue timestamp.
- Tellers use a distinct oldest-first `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` proof queue. Only the teller assigned by claim-next may decide it. Rejection records a required reason and creates no receipt. Approval additionally requires `receipts:post`, preserves the selected invoice set and passes actual cash/approved withholding allocations to `ReceiptPostingService` under the durable `MANUAL_PAYMENT_PROOF` source key.
- Receipt posting rechecks optional submitted invoice lock versions under its existing source → customer → invoice → certificate → series lock order. Proof approval therefore cannot create a second receipt on retry, directly change an invoice, consume a document number outside the receipt transaction, or bypass withholding capacity checks. The successful decision creates immutable receipt/audit/artifact history and an in-app notification; Reverb refresh is best-effort and carries no private financial payload.
- Added a role-filtered **My Bills & Payments** workspace for customers to choose one or more current bills, privately upload a new proof, submit it and follow/re-submit its review history. The **Payment Proof Review** workspace lets tellers or administrators claim the oldest proof, securely download the private proof with their authenticated session, record bank-confirmed allocations/reference, reject with a customer-visible reason, or approve the atomic receipt. The browser is never trusted to decide access, amounts, proof state or receipt posting.
- No gateway, payment threshold, deadline clock, automatic PPA clearance, SMS delivery or physical printing was introduced. A proof is not a paid state until its teller approval posts the collection receipt.
