# P0-05: User types and customer payment workflows

Recorded 2026-09-17; W20-W34 lifecycle, Studio, tariff/fuel, late-charge, transactional-SMS, registration, PWA and announcement improvements approved as design on 2026-09-19. The [customer service lifecycle](CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md) governs request queues, tax evidence, corrections, verified claims, payment policy/deadlines and shared Reverb updates/chat. [VIP credit](VIP_CREDIT.md) governs separate late-charge allocation/notice rules, [SMS notifications](SMS_NOTIFICATIONS.md) governs SkySMS delivery eligibility/status, and [customer registration](CUSTOMER_REGISTRATION.md) governs user/account/contact/buyer/OTP authority. Its approved choices supersede earlier proposal wording; unresolved gateway-provider, identity-evidence, SkySMS and taxpayer-specific inputs remain listed below. No domain implementation is claimed.

## Roles and workspaces

| User type | Workspace and allowed actions | Default access boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | Register with full name, company / registered buyer name, email and mobile; verify contacts/OTP; submit billing requirements/tax evidence; see queue/corrections; enter a bill number for verified lookup/claim; view/select bills and pay by allowed route; upload manual proof; view OR/history and chat | Active explicit account link or expressly verified invoice access; a name/company/contact match or OTP alone grants neither account-wide access nor other customers' data |
| VIP client (W17) | Customer access plus select bills to charge to credit, view account aging and repay by bank transfer with receipt upload for an authorized reviewer's approval | Credit eligibility belongs to the linked business account; bank repayment remains available with gateway enabled; no self-approval of payment or self-granted limits |
| PPA user | Look up an authorized bill and validate whether its due has been paid; see current outstanding amount, payment status and confirmed receipt reference | Assigned verification scope; no payment posting, receipt-upload approval, invoice editing, gateway configuration, or general financial reports |
| Teller | Claim next eligible billing request, review/return documents with reasons, encode/issue invoices including walk-ins, verify bank evidence, view invoice/OR reports and customer chat | Assigned organization/locations; tax approvals, clearance, corrections and other sensitive actions require specific permissions |
| Administrator | Manage all application areas, users, roles, settings, gateways, reports, invoices and payments | Operating organization scope; transaction integrity, source verification, audit, and correction rules still apply |

Seed role keys `CUSTOMER`, `PPA_USER`, `TELLER`, `ADMINISTRATOR`. The PPA role is independent of an invoice's PPA fee or template routing. Staff role assignment must not allow a customer to grant themselves staff access. W25 requires registration and walk-in bill claiming; verify authority using an approved contact/code or staff-review workflow before granting access. Administrator-provisioned links remain supported.

Customer accounts are business records; login users are identities. W32 requires a versioned current buyer profile and verified contact points in addition to an explicit scoped `customer_user_links` FK/uniqueness contract. Registration captures full name for the portal person, company / registered buyer name for the business account, and email/mobile contacts; it does not prove taxpayer buyer identity or company authority. A valid login and knowledge of a billing number alone do not authorize access to someone else's invoice. Bill lookup preserves leading zeroes, is rate limited, and gives a generic unavailable response for unknown and unauthorized bills. Enforce the same access rule for payment attempts, files, downloads, and private notification channels. W25 approves verified-contact/one-time-code or teller-reviewed claims, but a registration/mobile-change code cannot be reused as a claim code. Exact identity evidence, delivery-channel verification and broader account/delegated-payer authority still need definition; a bill claim alone grants no account-wide access.

Proposed permission families extend W13: `portal.bills.view`, `portal.payments.start`, `portal.proofs.submit`, `ppa.payments.verify`, `payment_proofs.review`, `payment_proofs.approve`, `payment_proofs.reject`, `payment_gateways.manage`, plus existing `billing.*`, `receipts.*`, `reports.*`, and administration capabilities. These are server-enforced capabilities with ownership/scope checks. PPA receives a minimal verification response; bank evidence, customer documents, and unnecessary personal data are excluded.

## Teller-issued bill and customer lookup

Planned Vue navigation and minimum screen contents:

| Workspace | Screens | Primary actions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer | Registration/profile/contact verification, bill lookup/detail, payment checkout or upload form, submission/payment history | Maintain future profile/contact details under verification rules; enter billing number, inspect authorized amount due, pay or upload, see pending/approved/rejected status and authorized issued receipt |
| PPA | Bill verification search/detail, own check history | Look up bill within assigned scope, refresh confirmed payment status, record check |
| Teller | Invoice list/editor, manual collection, payment-verification queue/detail, billing/collection reports | Encode/issue, record tender, inspect evidence and reference, approve/reject with reason, view permitted reports |
| Administrator | All authorized operational screens plus users/roles, settings, payment configuration, audit and reconciliation | Manage access, configure provider/offline instructions, inspect unapplied confirmed funds and exceptions, execute authorized corrections |

Teller verification queue shows bill, customer, claimed amount, method/reference, submission time and review status; review detail also shows current invoice balance, prior applications and possible duplicate warnings. Show the specific validation conflict if approval cannot post. PPA receives a smaller response than teller review. Customer messaging distinguishes `Awaiting verification`, `Payment processing`, `Partially paid`, `Paid`, `Rejected proof`, and `Payment received - reconciliation pending` where applicable. A fully paid bill offers receipt/history instead of a new payment action.

Proposed API groups under `/api/v1`: `/auth/register`, `/auth/contact-verifications/*`, `/portal/profile`, `/portal/bills/lookup`, `/portal/bills/{id}/payment-attempts`, `/portal/bills/{id}/payment-proofs`, `/portal/payment-history`, `/payment-proofs/{id}/approve` and `/reject`, `/ppa/bills/lookup`, `/ppa/verifications`, and `/admin/payment-gateways`. Exact methods/payloads will be frozen with OpenAPI; registration/claim requests return opaque generic results and mutations require the established command/version contract. Provider webhook routes use provider authentication and replay checks independently of browser session/CSRF routes. General invoice/receipt/report APIs keep their existing staff permissions.

1. Teller encodes a draft and posts it using the existing planned invoice/numbering transaction. Only a posted, payable invoice is offered for customer payment.
2. Customer signs in and enters the billing number. The API verifies linked customer account, organization, invoice state, currency, and current outstanding balance.
3. API returns the current bill and available payment route. For selected bills, create one account/currency payment group with explicit allocations. Enforce approved W23 server-side: combined gross remaining balances before this group's withholding, excluding fees, strictly below the configured threshold and gateway enabled. Otherwise use manual payment. Initial regular-customer checkout selects full remaining balances, not user-entered partial amounts; actual partial funds and VIP repayment retain their separate settlement/review contracts. Hiding a button alone is insufficient.
4. Both customer and PPA views obtain current settlement status from posted receipts and effective allocations. Pending evidence and unfinished checkout do not reduce the balance.

## Gateway enabled and selection eligible: online payment

1. Create a durable payment attempt with payment-group/customer/scope, explicit invoice allocations/versions, server-approved cash amount and separately approved withholding allocations, currency, provider account/environment, policy version, and a unique command key. Initial provider choice is undecided.
2. Call the provider outside the financial database transaction using a stable provider idempotency key where supported. On a timeout, reconcile the same attempt before creating another; a lost response does not establish failure.
3. Redirect the customer to provider checkout. Returning to a success URL displays processing until server verification completes; browser parameters never post a receipt.
4. Verify the provider's documented signed webhook or authenticated server confirmation, account/environment, event identity, transaction identity, amount, currency, and invoice mapping. Exact protocol belongs to the selected provider adapter and must be verified against its official contract before implementation.
5. Persist/deduplicate verified events and confirmations durably. Use a unique provider-account/environment/transaction key as well as event deduplication, since different events can describe the same payment. Post receipt, tenders, allocations, source link, audit and outbox through the shared receipt action with invoice locks and one atomic application of that confirmed transaction.
6. Provider-confirmed money and application settlement are separate states. If a manual payment or another checkout already settled the bill, retain the confirmed payment as unapplied/reconciliation-required; never lose the confirmation, over-allocate, or claim that received money failed. Refund/credit policy is an open accounting decision.

Duplicate, delayed and out-of-order callbacks must not create another receipt or regress a successful payment. An invoice reversal, provider refund or dispute uses explicit reconciliation/correction handling; it cannot silently toggle a paid flag. Provider fees are recorded separately from the amount settling the invoice.

## Manual route: upload and teller verification

This route applies when the gateway is disabled or the selection does not qualify under the configured threshold. W17 preserves VIP bank repayment regardless of gateway enablement. W23 extends explicit multi-invoice allocation to regular customers; use the same source/receipt allocation contract as [VIP_CREDIT.md](VIP_CREDIT.md), without granting regular customers credit. W24's instruction deadline, separate verification/clearance states and late-funds handling are defined in [CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md](CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md).

1. Customer confirms selected bills and receives versioned bank instructions and an explicit deadline. Upload proof linked to that payment group, with claimed amount, currency, payment date, receiving account/method and transaction reference as applicable. This upload is evidence, not an issued system receipt.
2. Submission becomes `pending_review`. The customer sees awaiting teller verification; PPA sees the bill's existing confirmed balance and a pending-verification indicator.
3. Teller checks the actual payment against the receiving account/collection record, amount, currency, payer/bill mapping, reference and prior applications. An image alone is not confirmation. Approved or rejected decisions record reviewer, time and reason; rejected proof can be resubmitted as a new linked submission, preserving the original.
4. Financial approval follows actual-funds verification, check clearance for deposited checks and applicable withholding review. Lock the submission, account, invoices and certificate capacity in the shared documented order, recheck balance/source uniqueness, then post receipt/tenders/allocations and the approved decision/source link atomically. Failure leaves the submission unapproved. Repeated clicks, two tellers and gateway/manual races must not apply the same money twice. Under approved W24, document-valid but uncleared checks remain pending, without final receipt application or paid status. When a VIP payment covers a W30 late charge, allocate it explicitly to principal and/or charge under the captured policy; withholding does not automatically settle the late charge.
5. Rejection changes only the proof-review state. Approval recognizes only the verified amount; partial settlement must remain partial. Approved proof cannot be edited/rejected to undo its payment; the normal receipt correction workflow applies.

Support teller-recorded manual payment without a customer upload through the same receipt action. Keep tender verification, proof review and invoice correction permissions distinct. A teller may encode a bill and later collect it as requested; requester/approver separation still applies to financial correction requests. When staff submit proof on a customer's behalf, proposed policy requires another authorized reviewer; finalize this with operations.

Uploads use private storage and authorized short-lived access, file count/size and content-type validation, content scanning/quarantine, and non-executable serving. Reference duplication and file hashes flag possible reuse; a hash alone cannot prove the underlying transfer is unique. Source identity must also prevent one transfer being posted through both manual entry and proof approval. Unknown/ambiguous references enter review instead of silently accepting a duplicate. W17's proposed multi-bill transfer contract uses explicit per-invoice allocation rows and one atomic receipt; see [VIP_CREDIT.md](VIP_CREDIT.md).

## PPA payment validation

W17 adds a separate **On credit** indicator for VIP bills. W18 makes PPA credit acceptance configurable in Admin Settings: require full payment or accept qualifying credit under the effective policy. Re-query current credit assignment, settlement and policy together; return clearance eligibility separately from payment status. Formal clearance issuance/release actions still need their own operational definition.

The initial interpretation is a verification screen showing bill number, relevant identity, original due, confirmed applied amount, remaining balance, settlement state, receipt reference, and checked-at time. Invoice lifecycle is shown separately: a reversed bill is not presented as a successfully paid active bill. Pending proof, failed/pending checkout, partial payment, and unapplied provider funds do not satisfy full-payment validation. Re-query the API at validation time; Reverb may refresh the UI but is not settlement authority.

Audit PPA checks. If PPA must issue a formal clearance, release cargo, or approve a gate pass, model that as a separate authorized workflow with its own record, validity and reversal behavior. That additional workflow has not been requested or confirmed.

## W31 settlement notification boundary

SkySMS is supplementary to portal/Reverb state. Payment instructions can create an eligible SMS intent after the group/deadline commits; proof upload and a gateway browser return never do, because neither proves money. A correction-needed/rejected proof, cleared/posted settlement, partial settlement, receipt availability, receipt reversal and reconciliation-required result are eligible only after their owning state commits. Keep bank details, full balance, proof image, withholding/tax detail and document link out of the message; the portal provides protected detail.

The shared receipt action creates the financial records, audit and notification intent atomically, then the worker sends outside locks. A `sent`, `pending`, failed or unknown provider status does not change payment, receipt, PPA or VIP state. For timeout/ambiguous SkySMS normal-send results, reconcile instead of automatic resend. See [SMS notifications](SMS_NOTIFICATIONS.md) for event defaults, template/contact policy and provider state.

## Configuration and persistence candidates

Administrator-managed Payments settings include gateway enablement, threshold/currency, provider and sandbox/live mode, credential status/rotation, receiving accounts/instructions, manual deadline hours/reminders, separate review/clearance targets and proof upload limits. Display W23's fixed v1 calculation basis and strict less-than comparator; changing their meaning requires an explicit future policy decision. W20 tax review and W21 billing-document requirements use their owning workflows. Secret material follows W14 write-only secret handling. Enablement requires validated provider configuration; record actor and version. Proposed initial default is gateway disabled until configured.

Disabling a gateway stops new checkouts while verified callbacks, reconciliation and existing attempts continue to completion. Enabling it does not discard pending uploads. Threshold changes affect new instructions and must not invalidate received money or shorten existing deadlines. Gateway outages alone do not automatically open another simultaneous payment route; enabled-but-unavailable checkout shows a retry/support state with attempt reconciliation.

Logical table candidates: `customer_user_links`, `payment_gateway_accounts`, `payment_attempts`, `provider_payment_events`, `provider_payments`, `payment_proofs`, `payment_proof_files`, `payment_proof_reviews`, and `ppa_verification_events`. Keep invoice/customer/user/receipt links as real scoped FKs, supporting indexes, source uniqueness, optimistic versions and protected history. Reuse a typed payment-source record across manual, proof and gateway posting so duplication checks cross channels. No table or migration is created by this document.

## Phase delivery and acceptance

Phase 1 seeds all four roles, scoped customer-account linking and route/API restrictions. P1-09 adds transactional-SMS contacts/preferences/templates/delivery foundation; P1-10 adds required registration/buyer-profile/contact/OTP controls; P1-11/P1-12 establish the safe PWA/announcement shell. Phase 2 supplies teller invoice issuance with immutable buyer snapshots and P2-11 attaches eligible request/invoice/tax/claim intents. Phase 3 adds customer bill lookup, proof upload/teller decisions, gateway adapter and reconciliation, manual collections, PPA verification and P3-12 settlement/VIP SMS delivery on the shared settlement model. Phase 4 expands teller reports and operational parity. See P3-05 through P3-07 in [PHASES.md](../PHASES.md).
W17 extends Phase 1 with account-specific VIP capability and Phase 3 with P3-08/P3-09/P3-11 for credit charging, authorized bank-proof collection, principal aging, late-charge policy/assessment and monitoring.
W20-W27 add P1-07/P1-08 upload/realtime foundations, P2-07 through P2-09 requests/tax evidence/verified claims and P3-10 payment policy/deadline/clearance. Withholding capacity and late-evidence accounting extend P3-01/P3-02; issued-unpaid corrections extend P3-03. These tasks remain planned.

Required acceptance includes customer A attempting customer B's bill/file/payment; PPA attempting posting or proof approval; enabled/disabled/misconfigured gateway routes; switching settings with in-flight payments; spoofed return URL; invalid, replayed, reordered and delayed callbacks; two tellers approving one proof; gateway confirmation racing manual payment; reused transfer evidence; upload quarantine; rejection/resubmission; partial payment; reversal after a prior PPA check; and agreement of customer/teller/PPA balances. Provider sandbox and operator acceptance are separate from automated checks.

Remaining inputs/decisions: provider; exact claim identity evidence and broader account/delegated-payer authority; applicable withholding rules and late-certificate accounting; bank-source matching; PPA lookup scope; staff-submitted evidence review separation; exact fiscal correction documents/tax effects; provisional check acknowledgments and excess/refund/dispute treatment; and W30 contract/fiscal/GL/late-charge allocation treatment. VIP allocation equations remain subject to their own review. Actual thresholds, deadline/correction-window hours, credit values, fuel inputs and late-charge values are Admin setup data. W23's aggregate basis/strict comparison/full-balance initial regular checkout, W24's instruction-start and clear-check-first rules, W26's linked-correction boundary, and W30's separate non-compounding-by-default late-charge ledger are approved, not open questions. Partial-entry regular checkout or additional daily/original-invoice limits require a future policy decision. PPA remains verification-only; formal clearance/release is outside the requested workflow.
