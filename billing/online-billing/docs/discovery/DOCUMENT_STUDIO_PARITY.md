# Document Studio parity and exclusion register

Task: P4-04. Updated: 2026-09-20. Status: **implementation mapping recorded; operator, fiscal, and physical-output acceptance remain open**.

This register maps the retained desktop document-designer and output workflows to the online Document Studio. It is deliberately narrower than a claim of operational, accountant, BIR, browser, or printer acceptance. It records where the online implementation intentionally changes the delivery mechanism so that a later migration or pilot does not silently reintroduce a workstation-local layout, a mutable issued output, or an inferred tax value.

## Evidence boundary

Desktop source reviewed for this register:

- `../Administrator/frmControlpanel.vb`: adds the Administrator's **OR Print Designer**, **Service Print Designer**, and **NSCL Service Print Designer** menu actions.
- `../DocPrint/OrPrintTemplateService.vb`: creates and saves a locally or UNC-configured `OfficialReceipt.repx`, binds it to `Receipt` and `Allocations`, and marks `or_indiprint='Y'` after a print request.
- `../DocPrint/ServicePrintTemplateService.vb`: creates separate normal and NSCL `.repx` files, binds `Bill` and `Items`, and selects NSCL when an `it_ccode` begins with `NSCL` after trim/case normalization.
- Parent `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/DATABASE.md`, and `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`: document the legacy PPA branch, Crystal statement/transmittal output, printable-number and print-flag meanings.

Online evidence reviewed:

- `apps/api/app/Services/DocumentStudio/DocumentStudioService.php`, renderer and routing tests.
- `apps/api/database/seeders/DatabaseSeeder.php`: seeded service, NSCL, PPA, collection-receipt, account-statement, yellow-transmittal, and white-transmittal layouts.
- `apps/web/src/views/admin/document-studio/index.vue` and `apps/web/src/api/documentStudio.ts`: centralized Admin Studio, history, activation, and branding-assets surfaces.
- [Document Studio contract](DOCUMENT_STUDIO.md), [P2-04 artifact evidence](../evidence/P2-04.md), and [P4-02 statement/transmittal evidence](../evidence/P4-02.md).

The legacy database schema, stored procedures, Crystal report definitions, and installed printers were not executed or changed for this mapping.

## Explicit current exclusions and safeguards

| Item                                                                             | Current scope decision                | Reason and re-entry condition                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Automatic `.repx` or Crystal `.rpt` import                                       | Excluded.                             | DevExpress and Crystal layouts are not compatible with the reviewed JSON/Dompdf contract. Recreate an approved layout through a mapped, reviewed Studio draft; do not claim binary compatibility.                                        |
| Workstation-local active layouts and ungoverned shared-file replacement          | Excluded.                             | The online product requires organization-scoped immutable versions, activation history, private assets, permissions, and audit. A browser user cannot replace an issued document by overwriting a shared file.                           |
| Layout-side calculations, SQL, scripts, remote image URLs, or arbitrary HTML/CSS | Excluded.                             | The Studio is presentation-only. Pricing, tax, PPA, fuel, settlement, and fiscal identity remain server-authoritative snapshots.                                                                                                         |
| Inferred white-transmittal tax/income/withholding classifications                | Excluded pending accountant approval. | Receipt totals are not enough evidence to determine the historical report's fiscal meaning. Add a field only with a reviewed source/mapping and test fixtures.                                                                           |
| Physical printer/paper profile, silent printing, and paper-alignment parity      | Deferred under P2-05.                 | Current scope is server-rendered PDF, browser preview/download, immutable artifacts, and print-attempt audit. Re-open with device, paper, printer-profile, signed-sample, and failure-status evidence before enabling physical printing. |
| Use of a print flag as proof of issuance, payment, or paper delivery             | Excluded.                             | Financial posting/numbering and artifact generation have their own transactions. A print request cannot mutate a payment or fiscal state.                                                                                                |
