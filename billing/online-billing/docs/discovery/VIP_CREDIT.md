# P0-05: VIP credit accounts, aging and bank-transfer collection

User requirement W17, recorded 2026-09-17, extended by W30-W32 on 2026-09-19. VIP clients have normal customer access plus selection of their bills to charge to credit, aging visibility, later bank-transfer payment with receipt-image upload, review by tellers or other authorized staff, configurable aging-based late charges, eligible transactional SMS, and the same required registration/buyer/contact/OTP boundary as other customers. This document is planning evidence; actual policy values, fiscal treatment and provider/privacy inputs remain reviewed setup inputs.

W18 clarification: credit limits, payment terms, overdue restrictions and PPA acceptance of on-credit bills are configurable in **Admin Settings > Credit & Collections**, including a **PPA Clearance Policy** section. Their configurability is confirmed. Actual production values are setup choices, not unresolved requirements that block designing these features.

## Admin Settings contract (W18)

W19 fiscal boundary: configurable credit terms/due dates affect collection, not required invoice issuance or tax recognition. Both cash and credit service sales use the applicable invoice; later proof approval allocates a collection, not a second sale. VIP credit assignment is not a credit note. Preserve fiscal date, charge date, due date and payment date separately. Applicable recognition/transition rules require accountant review under [BIR readiness](../BIR_COMPLIANCE.md).

| Section | Configurable controls | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Credit limits | Default limit per currency, explicit capped/unlimited mode, permitted customer overrides, account hold | Current effective limit governs new charges; zero capped limit means no capacity; missing configuration never means unlimited |
| Payment terms | Number of calendar days, due-date basis (invoice date or credit-charge date), permitted client override | Charge captures policy version and calculated due date; later settings changes apply to new charges |
| Repayment-proof review target | Default 24 hours, editable in a published VIP credit policy | Each submitted proof captures the target; an overdue proof is flagged for staff. It is not auto-approved or rejected, and the original bill due date and principal aging continue. A corrected resubmission starts a fresh review clock while retaining original queue priority. |
| Overdue restrictions | Allow, warn, or block new credit; grace days; optional overdue-amount threshold; block credit-based PPA eligibility separately | Uses original due dates; grace does not reset aging; existing debt remains payable under every mode |
| Late charges (W30) | Enablement, policy/default or permitted client override, grace days, non-overlapping aging bands, fixed/percentage basis, cadence, cap/minimum, rounding, charge due-date rule, allocation priority, account effect, hold/opt-out and contract/reference | Captured policy governs an eligible credit assignment; assessments are separate receivable events and do not rewrite the invoice or reset its aging |
| PPA clearance policy | Require full payment, or accept a valid VIP credit charge; enforce active account, overdue and limit checks where configured | Returns clearance eligibility separately from payment state and shows the reason/policy used |

Proposed precedence is explicit permitted customer override, otherwise organization default. Each control shows its inherited/overridden source. Credit eligibility always requires an active linked account; overrides cannot bypass ownership or expand a user's permissions. Location-specific PPA rules, if needed, require an explicit scoped policy rather than duplicating an account-wide credit limit.

Administrators edit validated forms and view resolved policy, effective time and change history. Use `credit_policies.manage` for default policy changes, `credit_accounts.manage` for customer overrides, `credit_late_charge_policies.manage/publish` for W30 and `ppa_policies.manage` for clearance policy; seed these for Administrator. The regular proof-approval permission grants none of them. Save with expected version, actor, reason and before/after values; publish an immutable policy version with an activation time. The UI belongs in Admin Settings while persistence and enforcement belong to Credit/PPA domain services, not arbitrary generic key/value updates.

Terms already captured on a charged invoice remain fixed. A W30 late-charge policy is captured with each eligible new credit assignment and is not activated retroactively against existing debt without an audited/accountant-reviewed migration. Current effective limits, holds and overdue restrictions govern new charges; current effective PPA policy governs each new eligibility check. Lowering a limit below current exposure shows an over-limit account without rewriting invoices or undoing past collections. Policy publication and competing charge/clearance actions must use a consistent active version with transactional validation; stale client previews require refresh. Pending uploads remain reviewable and repayment remains available. Cache refresh and private notifications occur after commit, and enforcement rechecks authoritative configuration.

PPA policy accepts credit only for an actual valid credit assignment, not just a VIP badge or uploaded receipt. The API exposes separate `payment_status`, `clearance_eligible`, `reason`, `policy_version` and `checked_at` values. A qualifying bill can display **On credit - eligible under policy** while remaining unpaid. Prior checks retain their captured policy/result; subsequent checks use current settings and current balances. The initial unconfigured mode requires payment and denies credit-based eligibility until an administrator activates a complete policy. Issuing an official clearance document or executing cargo release remains a separately specified operational action.

Acceptance includes default/override resolution, zero versus explicitly unlimited limits, invalid terms/thresholds, unauthorized/stale edits, policy publication racing a charge/check, both PPA modes, grace-day boundaries without re-aging, limit reduction with existing debt, preserved invoice terms, and repayments while blocked. W30 additionally requires policy/band/cap/cadence boundaries, duplicate scheduler protection, payment/reversal/waiver cases and separate principal/charge reporting. Concrete limits, term lengths and late-charge values are entered in Admin Settings before enabling operational credit.

## Account and permission model

Present a **VIP client** customer type in administration and the portal. Implement credit eligibility on the customer business account, with a scoped credit profile; a role name alone must never grant borrowing capacity. Existing CUSTOMER access and W32 verified customer-user links remain required. Full name/company/e-mail/mobile registration or mobile OTP proves neither account authority nor borrowing capacity. Multiple authorized login users share the same account exposure and limits. A user linked to multiple accounts receives credit access only for accounts with an active profile.

Separate permissions for `portal.credit.view`, `portal.credit.charge`, `credit_accounts.view`, `credit_accounts.manage`, `credit_aging.view`, `credit_aging.export`, `credit_late_charges.view`, `credit_late_charges.preview/post`, `credit_late_charges.waive/reverse`, and `credit_late_charge_policies.manage/publish`. Reuse `payment_proofs.review/approve/reject` and receipt posting policies for payments. Teller and other staff can receive scoped approval and credit-monitoring permissions without becoming administrators. Ability to approve a payment does not grant ability to change limits/terms, create/waive a late charge, write off debt or grant oneself VIP status. Administrator manages profile eligibility and policy through audited actions. Waivers/reversals require reason, separation of duties and an accountant-reviewed correction path. Disabling VIP privileges prevents new charges while retaining statement access and the ability to repay existing debt.

## Customer journey

W20-W34, approved as design 2026-09-19: VIPs use the same [request/queue, tax-evidence, billing, payment-selection and realtime/chat lifecycle](CUSTOMER_SERVICE_LIFECYCLE.md). VIP status grants no queue priority or tax exemption. W23's aggregate gross-balance threshold applies to new gateway selections; bank repayment remains available. Its initial regular-customer full-balance checkout rule does not silently replace this separate VIP allocation contract, whose equations still need review. W24 manual instruction expiry does not move original credit due dates, stop aging or erase debt; it is never itself a W30 late-charge trigger. W32 contact/OTP rules apply before a portal user gains access but do not grant credit authority; W33 PWA remains online for financial data; W34 announcements are non-financial. Customer history includes invoices, actual settlements, posted late charges, OR artifacts and permitted W31 SMS delivery status.

1. VIP logs in and selects one or more owned, posted, payable invoices to charge to credit. The screen shows each balance, applicable terms, proposed due date and projected account exposure.
2. Server revalidates account eligibility, invoice ownership/lifecycle, approved credit policy, existing charge assignments and current balances. Proposed default is to charge the full remaining balance of each selected bill; installments or split cash/credit require a separately specified policy.
3. Confirming records an idempotent credit-charge assignment for each invoice and a snapshot of the terms/due date, actor and time. Proposed multi-selection behavior is all-or-nothing with a clear failing-bill explanation.
4. Bill shows **Charged to credit - unpaid**, with due date and remaining balance. It remains the original invoice receivable: this action neither creates a second debt nor issues a payment receipt. Repeated charging cannot extend terms or duplicate exposure.
5. VIP selects credit bills to repay, supplies intended per-invoice allocation amounts, transfers money to the configured receiving account, then uploads the receipt image and payment reference/date/amount. A transfer may cover multiple bills from the same account and currency under the proposed allocation contract below.
6. Submission remains **Awaiting payment verification** until a teller or another user with the required scope and approval permission verifies actual funds and approves. Pending evidence leaves debt, aging and credit exposure unchanged.
7. Approval posts the verified receipt and allocations atomically, updates remaining exposure and derived aging, and shows the issued receipt and payment history to the VIP. Partial payment leaves an open balance; rejection preserves the debt and allows a linked resubmission.

VIP credit repayment by bank transfer and receipt upload is available regardless of gateway enablement. This is an explicit extension to W16's regular-customer fallback rule. Disabling a gateway or suspending new credit must not disable collection of existing credit debt. Existing gateway/manual payments against charged invoices also use the shared settlement action.

## Credit exposure and transaction rules

Profile/policy fields include enabled/held status, account/currency scope, reviewed limit policy and amount, payment terms/due-date rule, overdue-charge policy, captured late-charge policy version and contract/reference, effective version, and change audit. Missing terms/limit policy blocks new credit until configured; null must not silently mean unlimited. W30 permits late charges only when a complete published policy applies; actual numerical limit, term, rate, cap, cadence, fee/interest wording and automatic write-off values are never inferred from this plan.

Proposed exposure is the sum of remaining balances of active credit-charged invoices for this account/currency. If a numeric limit is configured, available credit is `max(0, limit - exposure)`, with excess exposure displayed separately. Uncharged payable bills are displayed separately, not hidden from the total customer receivable. Cross-location exposure aggregates according to the approved account scope; never give each location an independent copy of a shared limit.

Use a shared lock order for every charge/payment/reversal path: source command/receipt/proof as applicable, affected credit account rows in stable ID order, then invoice rows in stable ID order. Transaction-time reads enforce the limit across simultaneous selections and multiple logins. Charge confirmation records assignments/audit/outbox atomically; payment approval reduces debt only through posted allocations. Avoid holding these locks across provider calls or file scanning.

Credit exposure is reproducible from assignments plus the same invoices/allocations/reversals used by normal billing. Any cached balance is a projection updated transactionally and reconciled; it cannot authorize new credit independently. Receipt reversal restores the previously effective outstanding amount once, at the original due date. If reversal pushes exposure above a limit, flag/hold further charging under the approved policy; do not drop the valid reversal to make the limit look compliant. Credit-profile changes affect future charges, preserving existing terms snapshots. Re-aging existing debt needs a separate audited, authorized action.

## One bank transfer, one or more selected bills

Extend the payment-proof model with typed invoice allocation rows: submission ID, invoice ID, requested amount, and version/scope information. One proof may reference only invoices of the same linked customer account and currency. The server checks that requested amounts are positive, do not exceed current collectible amounts and do not exceed the verified supported settlement total. This proposal permits explicit partial allocations; exact withholding/short-payment rules remain P3-01 decisions.

Approval locks the source, account and invoices in the common order; checks source uniqueness across gateway/manual/proof channels; and posts one receipt with multiple allocation rows plus the reviewed decision in one transaction. If a selected invoice has changed or was paid elsewhere, return a review conflict with no partial approval. Preserve the actual received-funds record for reconciliation where receipt application cannot proceed. Do not silently redistribute money to other invoices or auto-select oldest debt. Changed allocations require an explicit, audited review decision and customer-visible result; automatic allocation policy remains open.

A duplicate image hash is a warning, not transfer identity. Match verified bank account/reference/date/amount using the approved reconciliation procedure. Different uploaded files must not permit the same transfer to settle twice. Overpayment/unallocated money is tracked separately for resolution, not silently deducted from another bill or netted against aging. Proof file privacy/scanning and review history follow CUSTOMER_PAYMENTS.md.

## Aging and account monitoring

Proposed buckets by days past the captured due date: **Current/not due** (as-of date on or before due date), **1-30**, **31-60**, **61-90**, **91+**. Use the business timezone/date and an explicit as-of date. W18 makes term length and invoice-date versus credit-charge-date origin admin-configurable; show the calculated due date before confirmation. Repeated charging cannot reset an existing due date. Calendar-day terms are the initial contract; additional month-end/business-day formulas need defined semantics before being offered.

For each invoice, age only its outstanding amount as of the selected cutoff. Include effective posted allocations/reversals through that cutoff; later payments must not erase earlier historical debt. Persist both effective business date and recorded timestamp and label the report's cutoff semantics, including backdated corrections. Bucket totals must reconcile to credit outstanding; total receivables also include separately shown uncharged bills. Group currency separately. Missing imported due dates appear as **Unclassified - needs terms review**, with their amount included in reconciliation; never silently put them in Current or drop them.

| View | Minimum contents |
| --- | --- |
| VIP portal / My credit | Current limit/available credit where configured, used credit, outstanding/overdue total, charged invoices, original due dates, aging buckets, pending proof and repayment history |
| Charge confirmation | Selected bills/balances, terms, due dates, total new exposure, remaining capacity and rejection reasons |
| Repayment | Selected bills/per-bill amounts, bank instructions, transfer reference/date/amount, image upload and review status |
| Staff credit accounts | Scoped customer account list/detail, credit status/terms/limit, principal versus late-charge totals, aging filters, pending reviews and permitted exports |
| Staff proof review | Actual received amount/reference, requested allocations, current balances, duplicate indicators, approve/reject/conflict result and reviewer audit |

PPA must see **On credit** separately from **Paid**, including remaining due where authorized. W18 resolves acceptance as an Admin Settings policy: require full payment or accept qualifying VIP credit. Eligibility does not relabel debt as paid or automatically execute a release action.

## Aging-based late charges (W30)

W30 is a post-due receivables lifecycle, not a tariff/fuel surcharge, commercial discount, withholding amount, payment-instruction expiry outcome or direct edit to an original invoice. It applies only to an eligible VIP credit assignment with a captured due date and complete captured late-charge policy. An account hold, dispute, correction, timely pending payment review or other policy condition can place an assessment on hold; a missing/disabled policy creates no automatic charge.

The baseline aging calculation is `days_past_due = max(0, Asia/Manila business date - captured_due_date)`: the due date is Current/not due and the next calendar date is day 1. A policy may define non-overlapping bands such as grace/0, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 91+, but exact bands are Admin setup data. A new policy does not backfill old debt or re-age a charged invoice. A later credit-term edit cannot reset the captured due date.

| Policy control | Approved boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility and effectivity | Organization default and permitted client override; currency/scope, effective date, account hold/opt-out and contract/reference. Capture the complete policy with each eligible future credit assignment. |
| Band and formula | Non-overlapping day bands, grace, fixed amount or controlled percentage, approved decimal basis, cadence, minimum/cap and rounding. No arbitrary formula editor. |
| Non-compounding baseline | Calculate against unpaid principal as of the run cutoff, excluding earlier late charges. Compounding is disabled unless a future explicitly approved policy and fiscal/accounting review authorizes it. |
| Collection effect | Policy declares allocation priority and whether unpaid late charges affect available credit/overdue restrictions. Show principal overdue, late-charge balance and total due separately. |
| Governance | Published policy, expected version, audit, maker/checker for waiver/reversal, customer notice text/version and accountant-reviewed fiscal/account mapping are required before operational activation. |

The scheduler creates an idempotent assessment keyed by credit-charge/invoice, policy version and cycle key. It locks the shared source -> credit-account -> invoice order, recalculates the current collectible principal and records cutoff, days, matched band, basis, rate, rounding, amount, policy version, effective time and recorded time. Assessment states are `CANDIDATE`, `ON_HOLD`, `POSTED`, `VOIDED`, `WAIVED`, `REVERSED` and `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`. A retry must return the same assessment rather than create another fee.

A posted late charge is an immutable, separately linked receivable/adjustment event. It does not rewrite invoice tariff, tax, due date, original aging or a collection receipt. Payment allocations identify principal versus late charge. Withholding does not automatically settle a late charge; P0-06 must approve any such treatment. A payment effective before an unposted assessment suppresses it. If a prior effective payment, correction, dishonor/reversal or dispute is found after a charge posts, use a linked reversal, waiver or reconciliation event with a reason and approved fiscal/accounting treatment; never delete or silently reprice it.

The customer sees the source invoice, principal, due date/days/band, posted late charges, payments, notice/policy reference and total due. Staff see the assessment/audit/reconciliation queue. PPA continues to see paid and on-credit separately; an unpaid late charge cannot silently redefine the original invoice as paid or unpaid without the relevant PPA policy result. W27 sends an after-commit account/aging refresh and durable new-charge notice where appropriate; W31 may create a separate eligible SkySMS intent only after a posted charge/waiver/reversal outcome passes the P3-11 fiscal gate. Balances remain server/API authoritative.

## Transactional SMS boundary (W31)

Eligible VIP messages are a credit assignment with captured terms/due date, an action-affecting account hold, configured due/aging reminder, confirmed/partial/rejected repayment, and posted late-charge/waiver/reversal/reconciliation result. Candidate late charges, holds, worker retries, current exposure recalculation, internal policy edits and PPA checks are never SMS events. The message is generic and portal-directed; it cannot include full credit/balance/tax/evidence data or treat a credit assignment as a receipt.

The credit/receipt command commits its assignment/allocation/audit/outbox before an SMS worker is allowed to call SkySMS. Contact verification/preference, template version, recipient scope, duplicate effect key and provider delivery state are independent of the credit ledger. `sent`/`failed`/unknown status cannot alter due date, aging, available credit, late charge, receipt or PPA outcome. See [SMS notifications](SMS_NOTIFICATIONS.md) for policy, status and resend rules.

Before P3-11 operational acceptance, the accountant/business owner must approve commercial authority/disclosure, charge wording, tax/VAT/percentage-tax/withholding treatment, fiscal document/series/template, recognition timing, GL/export mapping, allocation priority, waiver/reversal and dispute handling. Do not assume a late charge is interest, a BIR penalty, VATable/non-VAT, withholding-eligible or part of the original sales invoice.

## Persistence, phases and verification

Logical additions: `customer_credit_accounts`, `credit_policy_versions`, `invoice_credit_charges`, `credit_account_events`, `late_charge_policy_versions`, `late_charge_bands`, `late_charge_assessments`, `late_charge_events`, `late_charge_payment_allocations`, `late_charge_waivers` and `payment_proof_allocations`. Use real scoped FKs to customers/users/invoices/policies/proofs/receipts where applicable; unique active credit assignment per invoice and unique assessment cycle/policy/source; versioned terms and immutable audit. Include account/currency and source/due-date/cycle lookup indexes. Final migrations must avoid duplicating invoice monetary authority or creating a parallel payment ledger.

Phase 1 establishes the VIP account capability and distinct staff permissions; P1-09 establishes contacts/preferences/SMS delivery rules. P3-08 implements profile/charge/repayment and current credit monitoring after receipt/proof foundations; P3-09 implements reconcilable aging and historical cutoff behavior; P3-11 implements W30 policy, assessment, late-charge settlement, waiver/reversal and fiscal-output controls; P3-12 attaches eligible committed VIP delivery intents and status reconciliation. PPA acceptance includes on-credit status. P4-03 now provides an Administrator-only, organization-scoped, audited CSV of P3-09 principal aging at an explicit Asia/Manila cutoff. It does not include late charges, create a fiscal book, or issue PPA clearance. Phase 4 still extends statements/exports and communications operations, and Phase 5 reconciles imported credit classification/terms/open items without sending historical SMS. Unknown legacy VIP/term fields require source-owner mapping; never infer credit eligibility from a customer name or reconstruct historical terms from today's defaults.

Required future tests: regular customer/held VIP/other customer's bill rejected; simultaneous multi-login charges at the limit; repeated charge cannot duplicate debt or move due date; partial and multi-bill payment; proof pending/rejected leaves exposure intact; two authorized reviewers race; stale allocation plan and duplicate transfer across channels; gateway on/off with VIP bank repayment; receipt reversal restores original aging; profile changes preserve issued terms; aging on due date and at 1/30/31/60/61/90/91 days; historical cutoff with later or backdated payment/reversal; currencies and unclassified imported due dates; PPA distinguishes paid from on-credit; late-charge band/cap/cadence boundaries; grace and Manila-date boundary; scheduler retry/concurrent run; timely pending proof/hold; pre-run payment; post-run reversal/waiver/correction/dispute; separate principal/charge allocation/reporting and fiscal-output gate; and W31 verified/opted-out/stale contact suppression, atomic post-commit intent, provider unknown/no-blind-retry, posted-charge-only notice and no SMS-induced credit or receipt mutation. These are configurable fields, not hardcoded business decisions or design blockers. Remaining workflow decisions: shared account scope, whether each credit charge needs staff approval (default proposal: self-service within configured account policy), multi-bill/partial allocation details, excess funds handling, historical cutoff policy, late-charge fiscal/accounting treatment and any formal release action.
